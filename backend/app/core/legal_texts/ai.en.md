# How OurSport uses AI

- **Numbers never come from AI.** Scores, tables, schedules, draws, scenarios and predictions are computed by deterministic rules.
- **AI writes text from verified facts only.** Every story and commentary line is checked against the facts before it is published; anything that fails is held for an editor or replaced by a plain template line.
- **Always labelled.** AI-written text shows "Written by AI from match data"; text an editor checked also says "reviewed by an editor".
- **No betting, no odds.** Predictions are chances with ranges and reasons, never odds.
- **Children.** Hidden players are never named; junior coverage is encouraging and team-focused.
- **Setup.** AI reads uploaded rules and team lists into drafts; the organiser reviews everything and publishes.
