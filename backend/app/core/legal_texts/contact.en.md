# Contact and reporting

**Contact point (Digital Services Act).** privacy@oursport.example — answered in English, Finnish or Bengali.

**Report a story.** Every story has a report button (`POST /stories/{id}/corrections`): choose *mistake*, *privacy* or *illegal content*. An editor reviews each report; if a story is changed or removed you get the reason (statement of reasons) in your reports (`GET /me/reports`).

**Data requests about players.** Players and guardians contact the tournament organiser, who can export or erase participant data in OurSport.
