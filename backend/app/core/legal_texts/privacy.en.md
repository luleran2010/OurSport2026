# Privacy notice

**Who we are.** OurSport runs live results, stories and personal pages for local tournaments. For your account, follows and notifications OurSport is the controller. For team lists, players and contacts uploaded by a tournament organiser, the organiser's club or association is the controller and OurSport processes the data on its behalf.

**What we collect.** Your email, display name, language and time zone; the teams and tournaments you follow; your notification choices. For players: name, shirt number and birth year only. Players under 18 are hidden unless a guardian consents.

**Why.** To run your account (contract), to publish results and standings of organised competitions (legitimate interest), and, where you agree, for analytics cookies and marketing emails (consent).

**AI.** Match stories and commentary are written by AI from verified match data and are always labelled. No contact details or birth dates are sent to the AI service.

**How long.** Login links 1 day, notifications 90 days, scorer passes 30 days after a tournament, uploaded documents one year after it ends.

**Your rights.** Download your data (`POST /me/export`), delete your account (`DELETE /me`), correct your profile, object, or withdraw consent at any time. You can complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman (Tietosuojavaltuutettu).

**Contact.** See the contact page.
