Winter futsal cup, one day in the sports hall on two courts. Two groups of four, the top two of each group play the semi-finals, then a bronze match and the final. Win 3 points, draw 1 point, loss 0. Equal points: head-to-head, goal difference, goals, fair play, lots. 2 x 20 minutes with a 10 minute break, running clock. Five accumulated fouls per half; from the sixth foul a direct free kick. Saturday 14 November 9-20 on two courts.
