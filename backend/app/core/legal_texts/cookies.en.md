# Cookies

OurSport uses only essential cookies unless you agree to more.

| Cookie | Purpose | Kind | Lasts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `oursport_access` | Keeps you signed in | Essential | 15 minutes |
| `oursport_refresh` | Renews your sign-in | Essential | 60 days |
| `oursport_csrf` | Protects your actions against forged requests | Essential | 60 days |

Analytics cookies are set only after you allow them (`preferences.analytics_cookies`). You can change your choice at any time in your profile.
