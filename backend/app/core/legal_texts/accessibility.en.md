# Accessibility statement

OurSport aims to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA for the fan pages, the scorer pad and the organiser tools, in Finnish, English and Bengali.

**Known limitations.** Live pages update often; screen readers announce score changes politely, not every commentary line. Some printable sheets are PDF only.

**Feedback.** If something is hard to use, tell us on the contact page; we answer within 14 days. In Finland you can also contact the supervisory authority, the Regional State Administrative Agency for Southern Finland (saavutettavuusvaatimukset.fi).
