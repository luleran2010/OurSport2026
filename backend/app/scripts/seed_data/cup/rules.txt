FinBangla style cup. Four groups, top two to quarter-finals, bronze match. Win 3 points, draw 1 point, loss 0. Equal points: head-to-head, goal difference, goals, fair play, lots. 2 x 20 minutes with a 5 minute break. Friday 12 June 9-19, Saturday 9-19, Sunday 9-19 on four pitches; prayer break 13:00-13:30. Last year's semi-finalists are placed in different groups.
